About Chronos Archive
Founded on the belief that every memory deserves to be preserved with care — not just stored in a forgotten cloud folder.
✓ End-to-end encryption
✓ EXIF metadata extraction
✓ AI-powered organisation
✓ Timeline reconstruction
Photographs are more than pixels —
they are proof you lived.
Chronos Archive brings intelligence
and elegance to the way you
preserve what matters most.

Built on a modern stack with PHP, MySQL, and a beautifully crafted glassmorphism interface, Chronos Archive gives you the power of a professional archive studio — at your fingertips, on any device.

Everything you need

Crafted for memory keepers

🔒
Secure Authentication
Two-factor authentication, AES-256 encryption, and role-based access control keep your memories private and protected.
📤
Drag & Drop Upload
Upload hundreds of photos at once with a buttery-smooth drag-and-drop interface. Automatic compression preserves quality while saving space.
🤖
Smart Metadata
Automatic EXIF extraction captures camera model, GPS coordinates, aperture, shutter speed, and ISO — no tagging required.
📅
Memory Timeline
Watch your life unfold on a beautiful chronological timeline. Filter by year, season, or location for an immersive journey through time.
🔍
Advanced Search
Search by keyword, tag, date range, camera, location, or category. Find any photo in your archive in seconds.
🎞️
Slideshow Viewer
Full-screen cinematic slideshow with keyboard navigation, auto-play mode, thumbnail strip, and elegant transitions.
📊
Analytics Dashboard
Visualise your photography habits — upload frequency, storage growth, top categories, and most-used tags with beautiful charts.
🛡️
Backup & Recovery
Automatic hourly backups with versioning. Restore any file to any point in time. Cloud-sync to external providers.
⚙️
Admin Panel
Manage users, monitor storage, configure quotas, control permissions, and maintain your instance — all from one elegant dashboard.
